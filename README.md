> **Advancing cancer diagnostics through molecular science, liquid biopsy, epigenetics, and AI.**

Tvaster Genkalp is a molecular diagnostics company focused on developing innovative, blood-based solutions for cancer detection and monitoring.

Our work brings together **molecular biology, epigenetics, liquid biopsy, quantitative methylation profiling, and artificial intelligence** to support more timely and informed healthcare decisions.

---

## 🧬 What We Do

We develop diagnostic technologies designed to make cancer detection more accessible, minimally invasive, and clinically useful.

Our areas of focus include:

- 🧬 **Molecular Diagnostics**
- 🩸 **Liquid Biopsy**
- 🧪 **Epigenetics & DNA Methylation**
- 🤖 **AI-Driven Diagnostic Analytics**
- 🔬 **Cancer Detection & Monitoring**
- 🧠 **Clinical Research & Translational Science**

---

## 🩺 Episcreen® Liver

**Episcreen® Liver** is Tvaster's flagship blood-based diagnostic solution focused on **hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC)**.

The platform combines molecular biomarkers with clinical information and AI-driven analysis to support HCC screening, surveillance, and treatment monitoring.

Learn more:

👉 [Episcreen® Liver](https://tvastergen.com/)

## 🔬 Our Technology

Tvaster's technology platform is built around advanced analysis of molecular signals, including DNA methylation patterns.

Our research and development efforts include:

**Liquid Biopsy → Molecular Biomarkers → Epigenetic Profiling → Quantitative Analysis → AI/ML → Clinical Insights**

We are continuing to develop and expand our diagnostic technology across cancer applications.
